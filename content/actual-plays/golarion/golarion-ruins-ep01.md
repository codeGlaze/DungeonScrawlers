# Ruins of the Vault

## Episode Summary

The Ruins & Relics Archaeological Expedition, sponsored by the Pathfinder Society, has been investigating pre-Earthfall sites across Varisia. Led by veteran archaeologist Professor Aldara Vinthen, the team has discovered what appears to be an intact Thassilonian vault hidden beneath a seemingly mundane hillock near Sandpoint.

What begins as a routine archaeological dig quickly becomes a dangerous delve into ancient magic when the team awakens the vault's dormant guardians and discovers that some Thassilonian secrets were buried for very good reasons.

## Key Events

- **The Discovery**: Ground-penetrating divination reveals a massive structure beneath the hill
- **Breaking the Seal**: Ancient Thassilonian wards are carefully dismantled by Valdris
- **The Guardian Awakens**: A preserved Thassilonian mage construct challenges the intruders
- **Vault Exploration**: The team maps the first level of a multi-story magical laboratory
- **The Sinspawn Encounter**: Evidence of sinful magic experimentation becomes terrifyingly real
- **Session Climax**: Discovery of a functional runeforge still connected to ancient power sources

## Characters

## Locations Explored

### The Vault Entrance
A magically concealed portal built into living stone, featuring intricate Thassilonian runes that pulse with residual magical energy. The entrance required both arcane knowledge and precise ritual work to open safely.

### Hall of Mirrors
The first chamber beyond the seal, lined with perfectly preserved scrying mirrors that show distorted reflections of the viewers. Valdris determined these were used for divination magic and possibly communication between Thassilonian cities.

### The Construct Guardian Chamber
A circular room dominated by a massive stone guardian bearing the seven-pointed star of Thassilon. The construct challenged intruders in perfect ancient Thassilonian before engaging in combat.

### Laboratory Levels
Multi-tiered magical workshops filled with alchemical apparatus, spell components, and research notes written in ancient script. Evidence suggests this was a research facility for the Runelords' magical experiments.

## Combat Encounters

### Thassilonian Guardian Construct (CR 7)
**Location**: Guardian Chamber  
**Abilities**: Spell resistance, immunity to most magic, powerful slam attacks  
**Tactics**: Opened with *dispel magic* before closing to melee range  
**Resolution**: Defeated through coordinated attacks exploiting its limited mobility

### Sinspawn Swarm (CR 4)
**Location**: Lower Laboratory  
**Abilities**: Fast healing, sin-based magical abilities tied to wrath  
**Tactics**: Attempted to separate party members and focus fire  
**Resolution**: Seelah's divine channeling proved crucial in the victory

## Discoveries & Lore

### Ancient Research Notes
Valdris translated several key documents revealing this vault was dedicated to research into "sin magic" - the manipulation of mortal emotions and desires for arcane power.

### Thassilonian Runic Arrays
The vault contains several intact examples of Thassilonian magical theory in practice, including:
- Permanent *teleportation circles* linking to other research sites
- Embedded *continual flame* spells providing eternal illumination
- Preservation fields that have kept organic materials intact for millennia

### The Runeforge Connection
Most significantly, the party discovered that this vault contains a functioning connection to one of the legendary Thassilonian runeforges - workshops capable of creating incredibly powerful magical items.
