# Golarion Adventures

Welcome to our Pathfinder campaigns set in **Golarion**, the official campaign setting for the Pathfinder Roleplaying Game. This diverse world offers everything from the cosmopolitan nation of Absalom to the demon-haunted Worldwound, providing endless opportunities for adventure.

## Campaign Setting

**Golarion** is a world of high fantasy, featuring diverse cultures, ancient mysteries, and powerful magic. The setting is known for its:

### Major Regions

- **Absalom** - The City at the Center of the World
- **Varisia** - Land of ancient Thassilonian ruins and frontier settlements
- **Cheliax** - A devil-bound empire of law and tyranny  
- **Ustalav** - A gothic nation of haunted moors and undead
- **The River Kingdoms** - Lawless lands of bandit kings and mercenary companies
- **Osirion** - A desert nation of pharaohs and ancient pyramids

### Historical Ages

- **Age of Creation** - The birth of the world and first civilizations
- **Age of Serpents** - Rule of the serpentfolk empire
- **Age of Legend** - Rise and fall of great empires like Thassilon
- **Age of Darkness** - The meteorite impact and Earthfall
- **Age of Anguish** - Recovery from the great catastrophe  
- **Age of Destiny** - Classical antiquity and the rise of modern nations
- **Age of Enthronement** - The current era of mortal kingdoms
- **Age of Lost Omens** - The recent disappearance of prophecy

## Notable Features

### Magic & Technology
Golarion blends high fantasy with emerging technology, featuring everything from traditional wizardry to early firearms and clockwork constructs.

### Diverse Cultures
The world draws inspiration from many real-world cultures while maintaining its own unique identity, creating rich opportunities for roleplay and exploration.

### Ancient Mysteries
Ruins of lost civilizations dot the landscape, each holding secrets, treasures, and dangers for bold adventurers to discover.
