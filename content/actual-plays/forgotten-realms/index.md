# Forgotten Realms Campaigns

Welcome to our collection of actual play campaigns set in the Forgotten Realms, the iconic campaign setting of Dungeons & Dragons. From the bustling streets of Waterdeep to the dangerous depths of the Underdark, our adventuring parties explore the rich tapestry of Faerûn.

## Active Campaigns

### The Delvers of the Deep
A group of adventurers exploring ancient ruins and forgotten dungeons across the Sword Coast.

**Current Story Arc:** The Burnt Halls Investigation  
**System:** D&D 5th Edition  
**Players:** Kael (Human Fighter), Lyra (Elf Wizard), Thorin (Dwarf Cleric), Zara (Halfling Rogue)

## Campaign Setting

The **Forgotten Realms** is a campaign setting for the Dungeons & Dragons fantasy role-playing game. It is a detailed, living world with a rich history spanning thousands of years. Key locations include:

- **Waterdeep** - The City of Splendors, a major trading hub
- **Baldur's Gate** - A city of intrigue and adventure
- **Neverwinter** - The Jewel of the North
- **The Underdark** - A vast network of caverns and tunnels
- **Cormyr** - A kingdom of knights and nobility
