# The Burnt Halls

## Episode Summary

The adventuring party known as the Delvers of the Deep receives word of strange fires erupting from the ruins of Khaz Ankor, an ancient dwarven stronghold buried beneath the Sword Mountains. Local miners report that the fires burn without fuel and seem to move with purpose through the abandoned halls.

Hired by the Waterdeep City Watch to investigate these supernatural occurrences, our heroes delve into the forgotten depths to uncover the source of the magical flames.

## Key Events

- **Opening Investigation**: The party questions local miners and discovers the pattern of the fires
- **Descent into Khaz Ankor**: First exploration of the dwarven ruins reveals intricate stonework and ancient traps
- **Fire Elemental Encounter**: The party faces their first fire elemental in the Great Hall
- **The Forge Discovery**: Evidence points to the ancient forge as the source of the disturbance
- **Cliffhanger Ending**: A mysterious figure is glimpsed in the depths as the session ends

## Characters

### Player Characters

- **Kael Ironwright** (Alice) - Human Fighter, former city guard seeking adventure and purpose
- **Lyra Moonwhisper** (Bob) - Elf Wizard specializing in evocation magic and ancient history
- **Thorin Stonebeard** (Charlie) - Dwarf Cleric of Moradin, drawn to restore his people's lost heritage
- **Zara Lightfinger** (Diana) - Halfling Rogue and former street thief with a talent for finding trouble

### NPCs Introduced

- **Captain Marcus Thorne** - Waterdeep City Watch captain who hired the party
- **Old Henrik** - Grizzled miner who first reported the fires
- **Balin Goldbeard** - Dwarven historian providing background on Khaz Ankor

## Locations Explored

### The Outer Halls
Ancient dwarven corridors with masterwork stonework, though partially collapsed from age and the mysterious fires. Intricate geometric patterns carved into the walls tell stories of dwarven craftsmanship.

### The Great Hall
A vast ceremonial chamber with towering stone pillars. This is where the party encountered their first fire elemental, leading to an intense battle that tested their teamwork.

### The Forge Approach
Passages leading deeper into the mountain, growing warmer as the party approaches the heart of Khaz Ankor. The stonework here shows signs of more recent disturbance.

## Combat Encounters

### Fire Elemental (CR 5)
**Location**: Great Hall  
**Tactics**: The elemental used the pillars for cover and attempted to corner individual party members  
**Key Moments**: Lyra's *Frost Bolt* proved especially effective, while Thorin's healing kept the party in the fight

## Discoveries & Lore

- **Ancient Dwarven Script**: Thorin translated inscriptions revealing Khaz Ankor was once famous for its magical forges
- **Fire-Touched Stone**: Several stones throughout the ruins bear scorch marks that radiate heat but don't burn
- **The Forge Legend**: Local lore speaks of a legendary forge that could bind elemental fire into magical items

## Notable Moments

- Zara's failed attempt to disarm a pressure plate trap led to a comedic sequence of tumbling stones
- Kael's intimidation of the fire elemental surprised everyone at the table
- Lyra's encyclopedic knowledge of dwarven history proved invaluable for understanding the ruins
- Thorin's emotional reaction to seeing the desecrated halls of his ancestors added depth to the story

## House Rules & Mechanics

- **Inspiration**: Given for excellent roleplay and creative problem-solving
- **Flanking**: Used optional flanking rules for tactical combat
- **Environmental Hazards**: Heat exhaustion saves required in areas close to fire elemental activity

## Next Session Preview

The party has discovered that the fires originate from deeper within Khaz Ankor, likely from the legendary forge itself. With a mysterious figure spotted in the depths and growing elemental activity, the Delvers must decide whether to press on or retreat to gather more information.
