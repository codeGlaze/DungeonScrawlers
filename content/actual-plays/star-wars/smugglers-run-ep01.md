# Tatooine Gambit

## Episode Summary

The crew of the modified YT-1300 light freighter *Stardancer* is laying low on Ryloth when they receive word of a lucrative cargo job through their Twi'lek contacts. A simple run to Tatooine carrying "agricultural supplies" for a substantial payday - what could go wrong?

Everything, as it turns out. What begins as routine smuggling quickly escalates when the cargo turns out to be more than advertised, Imperial forces are tracking the shipment, and the drop-off location in Mos Eisley Cantina becomes the center of a three-way confrontation between the crew, bounty hunters, and Imperial Intelligence.

## Key Events

- **The Job Offer**: Vorn'ia Jess offers the crew 15,000 credits for a "no questions asked" cargo run
- **Hyperspace Complications**: Imperial Interdictor cruiser forces premature exit from hyperspace  
- **Tatooine Arrival**: Evasive maneuvers and false transponder codes get them past orbital patrol
- **Cargo Discovery**: B4-NK's scans reveal the "agricultural supplies" are actually rare minerals and encrypted data cores
- **Cantina Confrontation**: The buyer doesn't show, but Imperial Intelligence and bounty hunters do
- **Chase Through Mos Eisley**: High-speed pursuit through the streets on stolen speederbikes
- **Cliffhanger**: Escape to orbit, but the crew now has a price on their heads

## Characters

### Player Characters

- **Kira Synn** (Iris) - Human Scoundrel and captain of the *Stardancer*, former Imperial Academy washout with a talent for talking her way out of trouble
- **Zeph'ani'salo "Zeph" ** (Jake) - Twi'lek Pilot with Force sensitivity he keeps hidden, the ship's primary pilot and mechanic
- **Jorik Thorne** (Kelly) - Human Guardian and the crew's muscle, ex-Republic commando with strong anti-Imperial sentiment  
- **B4-NK "Bank"** (Louis) - Modified Protocol/Scholar droid with a dry sense of humor and encyclopedic knowledge

### NPCs

- **Vorn'ia Jess** - Twi'lek information broker who provided the job
- **Agent Kallus** - Imperial Intelligence operative tracking the stolen data
- **Gorax the Finder** - Trandoshan bounty hunter working for the Hutt cartel
- **Wuher** - Mos Eisley Cantina bartender who becomes an unexpected ally

## Locations

### Ryloth Underground
The starting location where the crew operates out of hidden bases in the cavern networks beneath Ryloth's surface. Twi'lek resistance contacts provide jobs and safe harbor.

### Imperial Interdictor *Subjugator*
The massive Imperial vessel that forced them out of hyperspace, featuring gravity well projectors and a full complement of TIE fighters. The crew managed to avoid direct confrontation through clever navigation.

### Tatooine Orbit & Atmosphere
Desert world patrol patterns and the challenge of slipping past Imperial checkpoints using false identification and sensor jammers.

### Mos Eisley Spaceport
The infamous "wretched hive of scum and villainy" where the deal was supposed to go down. The crew learned why smugglers prefer this port - local authorities look the other way for the right price.

### Mos Eisley Cantina
The legendary watering hole became a battleground when multiple factions converged on the crew simultaneously. The diverse alien clientele provided both cover and complications.

## Combat Encounters

### Imperial Intelligence Ambush (CR 6)
**Location**: Mos Eisley Cantina  
**Participants**: Agent Kallus + 4 Imperial troopers  
**Tactics**: Attempted to take the crew alive for interrogation  
**Resolution**: Wuher's "accidental" power outage allowed the crew to escape in the confusion

### Bounty Hunter Pursuit (CR 4)  
**Location**: Mos Eisley Streets  
**Participants**: Gorax + 2 Rodian hunters on speederbikes  
**Tactics**: High-speed chase through narrow alleyways and market squares  
**Resolution**: Jorik's well-placed thermal detonator ended the pursuit dramatically

### TIE Fighter Intercept (CR 8)
**Location**: Tatooine Orbital Space  
**Participants**: 2 TIE/ln Fighters from orbital patrol  
**Tactics**: Standard Imperial intercept protocol  
**Resolution**: Zeph's piloting and the ship's hidden weapons got them to hyperspace

## Galactic Context

### The Galactic Civil War
Set two years after the Battle of Yavin, the Empire is still strong but showing cracks. Rebel activity is increasing, and independent operators like our crew find opportunities in the chaos.

### Imperial Intelligence Operations
Agent Kallus represents the Empire's more subtle approach to controlling dissent - tracking information rather than planets, following data rather than fleets.

### Outer Rim Economics
The episode showcased how the war economy creates opportunities for smugglers, with high-value cargo commanding premium prices despite the risks.

## Consequences & Hooks

### Imperial Interest
Agent Kallus now has files on the crew and will be tracking their activities. Their ship's transponder codes are compromised.

### Criminal Connections  
Success in completing the run (eventually) has earned them a reputation with Twi'lek resistance networks and potential future jobs.

### The Missing Data
The encrypted cores they carried contained intelligence about Imperial fleet movements - someone very powerful wants that information back.

### Local Heat
Mos Eisley's criminal underworld now knows about the crew, for better and worse. Gorax the Finder survived and holds a grudge.

## Next Session Teasers

The crew needs to lay low while heat dies down, but Vorn'ia has another job opportunity - this one involving a mysterious passenger who needs transport to the Core Worlds. Meanwhile, Imperial Intelligence is offering a substantial bounty for information about the *Stardancer* and her crew.

The question becomes: in a galaxy at war, can independent operators stay truly neutral, or will they be forced to choose sides?
