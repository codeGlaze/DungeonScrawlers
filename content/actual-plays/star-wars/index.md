# Star Wars Campaigns

Welcome to our **Star Wars** roleplaying campaigns! Set in the beloved galaxy far, far away, these adventures span multiple eras and game systems, from the Clone Wars to the sequel trilogy and beyond.

Whether using the d20-based SW5e system or Fantasy Flight Games' narrative dice system, our campaigns capture the epic scope, moral complexity, and adventurous spirit that makes Star Wars so compelling.

## Active Campaigns

### Smuggler's Run
A crew of independent operators navigates the dangerous politics and profitable opportunities of the Outer Rim during the Galactic Civil War era.

**Current Story Arc:** Imperial Entanglements on Tatooine  
**System:** SW5e (Star Wars 5th Edition)  
**Era:** Galactic Civil War (0-4 ABY)  
**Players:** Kira (Human Scoundrel), Zeph (Twi'lek Pilot), Jorik (Human Guardian), B4-NK (Droid Scholar)

## Campaign Eras

### Old Republic Era (25,000 - 1,000 BBY)
The ancient history of the galaxy, featuring the rise of the Jedi and Sith orders, the Mandalorian Wars, and the Old Sith Wars.

### Rise of the Empire Era (1,000 - 0 BBY)
The final years of the Galactic Republic, the Clone Wars, and the rise of the Galactic Empire culminating in the events of Episodes I-III.

### Rebellion Era (0 - 5 ABY) ⭐ *Current Focus*
The Galactic Civil War period covering the original trilogy timeline, featuring the struggle between the Rebel Alliance and the Galactic Empire.

### New Republic Era (5 - 25 ABY)
The aftermath of the Empire's defeat, the establishment of the New Republic, and the rise of new threats in the galaxy.

### New Jedi Order Era (25+ ABY)
The sequel trilogy period and beyond, featuring the First Order, the Resistance, and the restoration of the Jedi.

## The Star Wars Galaxy

### Core Worlds
The wealthy, cosmopolitan heart of galactic civilization including Coruscant, Corellia, and Kuat.

### Mid Rim
Stable, developed worlds that serve as industrial centers and trade hubs throughout the galaxy.

### Outer Rim Territories
The frontier regions where our campaigns typically focus - lawless, dangerous, but full of opportunity for those brave enough to seek it.

### Wild Space & Unknown Regions
Largely unexplored areas beyond the galaxy's edge, home to ancient secrets and alien civilizations.

## Game Systems

### SW5e (Star Wars 5th Edition)
A comprehensive adaptation of D&D 5th Edition mechanics to the Star Wars universe, featuring:
- Classes like Scoundrel, Guardian, Sentinel, and Consular
- Expanded rules for space combat and starship operations  
- Force powers adapted from D&D spell mechanics
- Equipment and technology appropriate to the Star Wars setting

### Star Wars RPG (Fantasy Flight Games)
The narrative dice system focusing on collaborative storytelling:
- Advantage/Threat mechanics encouraging creative problem-solving
- Obligation, Duty, and Morality systems for character motivation
- Detailed starship and vehicle combat
- Three core games: Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion, Force and Destiny

## Themes & Tone

Our Star Wars campaigns emphasize:
- **Heroic Adventure** - Players are heroes making a difference in galactic events
- **Moral Complexity** - Exploring the gray areas between light and dark, Empire and Rebellion
- **Epic Scope** - Adventures that feel like they belong in the Star Wars saga
- **Character Growth** - Personal journeys of discovery, redemption, and purpose
- **Diverse Perspectives** - Stories from across the galaxy's many species and cultures
